## Design Context

### Users
- **Requests & In Progress pages**: Broad marketing team and company-wide audience. Anyone at Homebase who needs to request web work or check on in-progress tickets. Non-technical, casual usage.
- **Dashboard**: Web team only. Data-savvy engineers and PMs analyzing sprint performance, velocity, cycle time. Frequent, analytical usage.

### Brand Personality
- **Voice**: Neighborly (helpful, relatable), Clear (direct, transparent), Proactive (confident, ready)
- **3-word personality**: Friendly, Confident, Crafted
- **Emotional goals**: Requests/In Progress should feel approachable and slightly playful. Dashboard should feel confident and analytical while still friendly.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual tone**: Light, clean, approachable. Not corporate, not generic SaaS.
- **Theme**: Light mode only. No dark mode.
- **References**: Homebase brand guide (https://homebase-brand-guide.vercel.app)
- **Anti-references**: Generic SaaS dashboards, dark mode with glowing accents, overly corporate/sterile

### Design Principles
1. **Brand-first**: Purple Haze (#7E3DD4) is always present. Spectrum gradient is AI-only. Plus Jakarta Sans is the primary typeface.
2. **Hierarchy through restraint**: Not everything can be bold. Use weight, size, and space to create clear hierarchy.
3. **Intentional decoration**: Every gradient, shadow, and accent must earn its place. Remove decoration that doesn't serve a purpose.
4. **Data clarity**: Charts and metrics need distinct colors with good contrast. Analytical pages prioritize readability over flair.
5. **Approachable confidence**: Feel helpful and human, not intimidating or cold. The tool should make people want to use it.
